Welcome to my little corner of the internet!

I made this website as part of a Hack Club Stardance project, but I didn't want it to feel like a typical portfolio. I wanted it to feel more like **me** — a digital scrapbook filled with things I like, things I've made, and random little pieces of my personality.

I'm 14, in 9th grade, and I love to write, read, draw, create things, and generally collect way too many interests. I like poetry, books, art, scrap journaling, music, astronomy, and basically anything that gives me something to think about or make.

So instead of making a very neat and professional-looking website, I went for something a little more chaotic. Brown and cream colours, paper scraps, flowers, butterflies, textures, handwritten-looking text, artwork, poetry, and all the little details that make a physical scrapbook feel personal.

What's inside?

You'll find some of my favourite things, my artwork, pieces of writing and poetry, books and music I like, and a little glimpse into the things that make up my own tiny universe.

It's not supposed to be a perfect representation of me — because honestly, I'm not sure there is such a thing. It's just a collection of things I love right now.

 Making the site

I built the website using HTML and CSS, experimenting with layouts, colours, positioning, textures, and ways to make a webpage feel more like a physical scrapbook.

I also used AI as a learning and brainstorming tool when I got stuck with coding or wanted ideas for certain effects. I edited, experimented with, and made the final design my own.

A little note

This website will probably change as I do.

I'll add new art, new writing, new books, new obsessions, and probably remove things and put them back again for absolutely no reason.

For now, though, welcome to my little mess of a website. ♡

— Manvi
